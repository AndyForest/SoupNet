# Options with Soup.net

A suggestion, for you to weigh. If you already have a way to put choices to a person, keep it. What Soup.net adds is a record of what they chose and why, which later agents can find.

**Why it can help.** Picking the likeliest reading logs your first instinct as their position. Showing two to four framings, each written as a full recipe with its own evidence for why it's a candidate, lets them see the claim they'd be standing behind. Their choice then carries more weight than your guess did.

**Recording it.** A check is a position the person holds, so a candidate checked before they choose records something not yet true. After they choose, check that one and add to its evidence that they saw N framings and picked it, in their words where they gave them. If none fits, that's useful too: new framings from what they said tend to land better than the closest miss.

**Other shapes.**
- If every option will really be pursued, each is a true claim: check them all as ordinary recipes under one intent, then log feedback once one wins.
- If the person isn't reachable and you can't stand behind any option, drafts under one intent can hold the question, with what would decide it stated first. `verify_draft` marks the losers `not_chosen`.

**Often not worth it.** The call is already logged and nothing new is on the table, it's small and easy to reverse, or the person asked you to decide.
